About Bubble

Bubble is a hyperlocal platform that helps people discover and build genuine community where they live. We're connecting neighbors with local events, groups, and venues in the Bay Area, starting with Berkeley, San Francisco, and surrounding communities.

Our Mission: Connection Over Clicks. We believe real community happens offline, in neighborhoods, through authentic human connection.

Bubble enables users to discover local activities, join community groups ("Bubbles"), RSVP to events, and book venue time slots. Founded with the belief that technology should bring people together offline, not replace genuine human interaction.
