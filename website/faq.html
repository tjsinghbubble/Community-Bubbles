Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bubble

Bubble is a mobile-first community platform that connects people through hyper-local events and groups. We make it effortless to discover activities in your neighborhood, meet verified members, and build lasting friendships through real-world experiences.
We verify members through campus email addresses (.edu), community affiliations, and identity verification. This ensures every person on Bubble is who they say they are, creating a safe and trustworthy environment for authentic connections.

Yes! Bubble is free for all members. Our mission is to combat loneliness and build community, making authentic connection accessible to everyone. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but the core experience will always be free.
Everything from pickup sports (pickleball, basketball, tennis) to dining groups, hiking adventures, study sessions, coffee meetups, creative workshops, and more. If you can think of it, you can create a bubble for it!
Absolutely! Creating a bubble is easy. Choose your interest, set the details (location, time, description), invite others, and watch your community grow. You can create both one-time events and ongoing groups.
Bubble focuses exclusively on real-world connection in your immediate area. We're hyper-local, verification-first, and built specifically for creating meaningful in-person experiences—not endless scrolling. Every feature is designed to get you offline and into the world with your community.
Safety is our top priority. Beyond member verification, we have clear community guidelines, instant reporting tools, dedicated moderation, and public event locations. We also encourage group gatherings and provide safety resources for all members.
