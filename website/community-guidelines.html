Community Guidelines

Last Updated: March 7, 2026

Bubble is built on trust, respect, and authentic connection. These guidelines help maintain a safe and welcoming community for everyone.

✅

Do:

  • Be Respectful: Treat everyone with kindness and respect, regardless of background, identity, or beliefs
  • Be Authentic: Use your real identity and be honest in your interactions
  • Show Up: If you RSVP to an event, make every effort to attend. Respect others' time
  • Communicate: If plans change, let organizers and attendees know as soon as possible
  • Stay Safe: Meet in public places, trust your instincts, and report any concerns
  • Be Inclusive: Welcome newcomers and create spaces where everyone feels they belong
❌

Don't:

  • Harassment: No bullying, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory behavior
  • Inappropriate Content: No sexual content, nudity, or explicit material
  • Spam or Solicitation: No unsolicited commercial messages or pyramid schemes
  • Fake Profiles: No impersonation or misleading information
  • Illegal Activity: No promotion of illegal activities or substances
  • Privacy Violations: No sharing others' personal information without consent
🛡️

Reporting & Safety

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these guidelines:

  • Use the in-app reporting feature immediately
  • Contact our support team at hello@trybubble.io
  • In case of emergency, contact local authorities
⚖️

Consequences

Violations of these guidelines may result in:

  • Warning and educational outreach
  • Temporary suspension of account
  • Permanent ban from the platform
  • Legal action when necessary

Remember: Bubble is about building genuine friendships and community. When we all follow these guidelines, we create a space where everyone can thrive. Thank you for being part of our community! 💙
