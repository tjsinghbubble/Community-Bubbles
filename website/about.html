About Bubble

Connection Over Clicks, Building genuine community where people live.

Our Mission

We believe real community happens offline, in your neighborhood. Bubble is a platform that helps people discover, create, and attend local events and communities. We're connecting neighbors with each other, one bubble at a time.

Started in the Bay Area

Bubble launched to help people in Berkeley, San Francisco, and surrounding communities find authentic connection with those around them. Our vision is to bring people together, not through endless scrolling, but through real experiences in real places.
