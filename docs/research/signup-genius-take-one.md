# SignupGenius External Analysis

**Company Overview**  
SignupGenius is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that automates the creation, distribution, and management of online sign-up sheets, primarily for volunteer coordination, event registration, and group scheduling. The service targets schools, churches, youth sports leagues, nonprofits, and small-to-medium businesses that require lightweight, low-cost coordination tools.

---

## Core Product Areas & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Sign-Up Sheet Creation & Management
- **Template Library**  
  - Pre-built templates for common use cases (e.g., potluck, carpool, classroom volunteer, tournament bracket).  
  - Ability to save custom templates for reuse across groups or seasons.
- **Sheet Builder**  
  - Drag-and-drop or wizard-driven interface for defining time slots, roles, quantities, and descriptions.  
  - Configurable fields: date/time ranges, location, max sign-ups per slot, waitlists, recurring events.
- **Bulk Upload & Import**  
  - CSV/Excel import of participant lists or existing schedules.  
  - Google Calendar and Outlook integration for date/time population.
- **Versioning & Cloning**  
  - Duplicate existing sheets for recurring events or parallel groups.  
  - Archive and restore historical sheets.

### 2. Participant Experience & Accessibility
- **Responsive Web Forms**  
  - Mobile-optimized sign-up pages with touch-friendly inputs and calendar pickers.  
  - ADA/WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility features (screen-reader labels, high-contrast modes).
- **Multi-Channel Notifications**  
  - Automated email and SMS reminders (sign-up confirmation, change alerts, event-day nudges).  
  - Customizable sender name, subject lines, and merge tags.
- **Social & Sharing Tools**  
  - One-click sharing via email, Facebook, Twitter, embedded iframes, and QR codes.  
  - “Forward to a friend” and referral tracking.

### 3. Administration & Coordination Tools
- **Dashboard & Reporting**  
  - Real-time headcount dashboards segmented by role, date, or location.  
  - Exportable CSV, PDF, and Excel reports; scheduled email delivery of reports.
- **Role-Based Permissions**  
  - Owner, co-organizer, and view-only roles with granular feature toggles.  
  - Sub-group creation for multi-team or multi-class scenarios.
- **Waitlist & Auto-Promotion**  
  - Configurable waitlist size and automatic promotion when slots open.  
  - Manual override and bulk move tools.
- **Conflict & Double-Booking Prevention**  
  - System-level checks preventing one participant from claiming overlapping slots.  
  - Optional “block-out” dates for key individuals (coaches, teachers).

### 4. Payments & Monetization Layer
- **Integrated Payments (Premium)**  
  - Stripe and PayPal Connect for event fees, dues, or donations.  
  - Customizable fee schedules, taxes, and processing-fee pass-through.  
  - Automated receipts and refund workflows.
- **Fundraising Add-Ons**  
  - Optional “add a donation” checkbox during sign-up.  
  - Pledge tracking and donor export for CRM import.

### 5. Data Management & Integrations
- **Contact & CRM Sync**  
  - Two-way sync with Mailchimp, Constant Contact, and Google Contacts.  
  - Zapier and Make (Integromat) connectors for 3,000+ downstream apps.
- **Calendar & Productivity Integrations**  
  - Bi-directional sync with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar.  
  - iCal feed generation for external calendars.
- **API & Webhooks**  
  - RESTful API endpoints for sheet creation, participant CRUD, and webhook event triggers.  
  - Developer documentation and sandbox environment (paid tier).
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**  
  - SAML 2.0 and OAuth2 support for school districts and enterprise customers.

### 6. Branding & White-Label Options
- **Custom Domain & Branding**  
  - Upload logo, color palette, and custom header/footer on paid plans.  
  - Vanity URL (e.g., signup.yourschool.org).
- **White-Label / Reseller Program**  
  - Full removal of SignupGenius marks; ability to resell under partner brand.  
  - Volume-based revenue-share tiers for larger organizations.

### 7. Compliance, Security & Support
- **Data Protection**  
  - SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA compliance documentation available.  
  - Data residency options (US-only storage) for education and government clients.
- **Account & Access Security**  
  - 2FA, password policy enforcement, session timeouts, IP allow-listing.  
  - Automated anomalous-login alerts.
- **Customer Support Tiers**  
  - Email/ticket support (free), priority phone & chat (paid), dedicated account manager (enterprise).  
  - In-app help center, video tutorials, and webinar training library.

### 8. Pricing & Packaging Architecture
- **Free Tier**  
  - Limited to 1 active sheet, 50 sign-ups/month, basic email notifications.  
  - Watermarked pages and SignupGenius branding.
- **Essentials / Premium Plans**  
  - Tiered monthly or annual subscriptions unlocking higher limits, payments, branding, and integrations.  
  - Add-on credits for SMS, extra sheets, or increased storage.
- **Enterprise Licensing**  
  - Volume seat or organization-level contracts with custom SLAs, SSO, and data-processing agreements.  
  - Onboarding & migration services bundled.

---

## Strategic Observations (External View)
- **Market Position**: Positioned as the “lite” alternative to full event-management suites (Eventbrite, Cvent) and volunteer platforms (VolunteerHub, SignUpGenius competitors such as SignUp.com and Doodle).  
- **Differentiation Levers**: Lowest-friction sign-up creation, deep education/nonprofit focus, and an expanding payments layer.  
- **Expansion Vectors**: Deeper vertical solutions (youth sports league management, church small-group tools), API-first growth for EdTech platforms, and white-label channels for school-district software resellers.
