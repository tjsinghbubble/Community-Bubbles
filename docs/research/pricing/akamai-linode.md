## Example price points

- **Linode 4GB (Shared, 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 80 GB SSD / 4 TB transfer): $24/mo** — fits the Node.js API VM spec exactly.
- **Dedicated 4GB (G7, 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 80 GB SSD): $43/mo** (G8 "Dedicated 4x2": $0.07/hr ≈ $51/mo, uncapped hourly).
- **Smallest Managed PostgreSQL (Shared 1GB, 1-node): $16/mo**; production-comparable Shared 4GB 1-node: $63/mo (3-node HA: $147/mo).
- **Object Storage base: $5/mo** including 250 GB storage + 1 TB added to the account transfer pool.
- Reference stack (VM $24 + PG shared 4GB 1-node $63 + Object Storage $5 + NodeBalancer $10) ≈ **$102/mo**; drop the NodeBalancer and shrink the DB to 1GB → ≈ $45/mo.

## Gotchas

- **Transfer is pooled account-wide, not per-instance.** Each service's allowance (4 TB for a Linode 4GB, 1 TB for Object Storage, etc.) joins one shared pool per region group; overage is a cheap $0.005/GB in US regions but jumps to $0.015/GB in Jakarta and $0.01/GB in distributed regions. Allowances are prorated by hours active.
- **Managed Databases have a messy history — check doc dates.** The 2022-era product was pulled from new sales in 2023 and relaunched in 2024 on Aiven infrastructure; older blog posts, prices, and API examples for "legacy" clusters are stale. Managed DBs also only deploy to *core* compute regions, not distributed ones.
- **Rebranding trap:** linode.com pricing URLs 301-redirect to akamai.com/cloud/pricing, and docs live at techdocs.akamai.com under "Cloud Computing." Third-party articles citing old linode.com prices (e.g., Dedicated 4GB at $36/mo, extra IPv4 at $1/mo) predate the 2025 price adjustments — current figures are $43/mo and $2/mo.
- **New G8 dedicated plans bill pure hourly with no monthly cap** (as of Jul 1 2026), unlike classic plans whose hourly billing caps at the monthly price. An always-on G8 4x2 costs more than the capped G7 Dedicated 4GB.
- **Object Storage egress always meters**, even to a Linode in the same data center — put the app and bucket in the same region anyway for latency, but don't assume same-DC traffic is free like it is for private IPv4/VPC.
- **NodeBalancer is optional at this scale**: at <100 req/s a single VM with Caddy/nginx doing TLS is fine; Cloud Firewall and DDoS protection are free either way.
