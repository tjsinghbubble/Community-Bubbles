## Example price points (us-east, 730 hr/mo)

- **Always-on API VM — bx2-2x8 (2 vCPU / 8 GB, Ubuntu)**: $0.0916/hr → **$66.87/mo**; + 100 GB general-purpose boot/data volume $8.78 + floating IPv4 $4.38 → **≈ $80/mo** for the VM stack. (cx2-2x4, 2 vCPU / 4 GB ≈ **$53.86/mo** if 4 GB is enough; current-gen bx4-2x8 ≈ $74.45/mo.)
- **Smallest sensible Databases for PostgreSQL**: 2 members × (0.5 vCPU / 4 GB RAM / 5 GB disk) = **$82.54/mo**; a more realistic 0.5 vCPU / 4 GB / 40 GB-per-member deployment = **$126.96/mo** (matches IBM's own docs example of ~$126).
- **COS 50 GB photos + 100 GB egress**: 50 GB Smart Tier (hot) $1.14 + 100 GB egress @ $0.09 = $9.00 → **≈ $10.13/mo** (+ pennies for requests).
- **Code Engine alternative (always-on 2 vCPU / 4 GB, min-instances=1)**: ≈ **$213.5/mo** net of free tier — not competitive for a can't-scale-to-zero app.
- Rough total, VM path (VM + volume + IP + managed PG min + COS): **≈ $172/mo**; swap in ALB (+$21.57 + data) if you want managed TLS/LB instead of a reverse proxy on the VM.

## Gotchas

- **Databases for PostgreSQL bills everything ×2**: the minimum deployment is 2 data members (HA pair, not optional), and RAM/disk/vCPU allocations are per member — the "4 GB RAM / 5 GB disk" floor is really 8 GB RAM / 10 GB disk on your bill (~$82.5/mo minimum, before you add realistic disk).
- **Pricing opacity**: IBM publishes almost no unit prices on public web pages (marketing pages 403/JS-only); real numbers live in the console estimator (cloud.ibm.com/estimator) and the Global Catalog pricing API. Third-party pages are often stale, and 2024's regional "price uplifts" (up to ~26%) made old citations unreliable.
- **Code Engine punishes always-on**: with min-instances=1, 2 vCPU / 4 GB costs ~$214/mo vs ~$67/mo for the same class VSI. Its free tier (100k vCPU-s / 200k GB-s) is ~1.4% of an always-on month.
- **Floating IPv4 is no longer free**: $0.006/hr (~$4.38/mo) each, billed whether or not traffic flows; the free public gateway is outbound-only, so any inbound service needs a floating IP or a load balancer.
- **Egress stacks**: VPC internet egress (~$0.092/GB after a ~5 GB allowance) is billed by the VPC service *in addition to* the ALB's $0.00844/GB data-processed fee; COS egress (~$0.09/GB) is billed separately again unless you're on One-Rate.
- **Generation churn**: bx2/cx2 (gen2) profiles are still sold but are disappearing from the estimator's plan list in favor of bx3d/bx4; gen2 is priced per vCPU-hr + GB-RAM-hr parts while gen3/4 have flat per-profile hourly rates, so quotes for "the same" 2×8 box differ (bx2-2x8 $66.87 vs bx4-2x8 $74.45/mo).
